### Yanlış yanıtladığınız sorular
_Açıklamalar İngilizcedir._
- **A1-ERR-001**: We [are] from Spain, but now we [live] [in] [the] Paris.
  - Yanıtınız: live
  - Doğru yanıt: the
  - City names normally take no article, so 'the Paris' is wrong; say 'in Paris'. 'Are' is correct with 'we'. 'Live' is the correct present simple for where you live now. 'In' is the right preposition for cities.
- **A2-RDG-002** (A message from Ella): When does Ella want to meet Sam?
  - Yanıtınız: On Saturday
  - Doğru yanıt: On Tuesday next week
  - Ella writes, 'Can we meet next week instead? I'm free on Tuesday after work.' Saturday is the day of Sam's party, Friday evening is when her family leaves, and Sunday afternoon is when they come back.
- **B1-LEX-001**: My doctor told me to ___ smoking, and I haven't had a cigarette since.
  - Yanıtınız: give out
  - Doğru yanıt: give up
  - 'Give up' means stop a habit. 'Give in' means accept defeat or agree after pressure, and it does not take an object like 'smoking'. 'Give out' means distribute. 'Give away' means give something to others for free.
- **B1-CLZ-007**: She ___ a lot of mistakes in her first English essay.
  - Yanıtınız: had
  - Doğru yanıt: made
  - We 'make' mistakes. 'Did' is common with tasks and homework, but not with 'mistakes'. 'Had' and 'took' do not go with 'mistakes'.
- **B1-CLZ-004**: You ___ bring food into the museum; it's not allowed.
  - Yanıtınız: can
  - Doğru yanıt: mustn't
  - 'Mustn't' means it is not allowed, which matches the second half. 'Don't have to' and 'needn't' mean it is not necessary, but it is allowed. 'Can' means it is allowed, the opposite.
- **A2-RDG-003** (City Bikes): How much does it cost to rent a bike for a whole day?
  - Yanıtınız: 8 euros
  - Doğru yanıt: 20 euros
  - The text says 'a whole day costs 20 euros'. 5 euros is the price for one hour. 12 is the age in the helmet rule, and 8 is the opening time.
- **A2-RDG-005** (City Bikes): What kind of text is this?
  - Yanıtınız: A list of city hotels
  - Doğru yanıt: An advert for renting bikes
  - The text tells readers what the shop offers, the prices and the opening times, so it is an advert. It is not written to a friend, it reports no race, and it lists no hotels.
- **A2-LEX-001**: It's cold outside, so ___ your jacket before you go.
  - Yanıtınız: put off
  - Doğru yanıt: put on
  - 'Put on' means start wearing clothes. 'Take off' is the opposite: remove clothes. 'Put off' means delay something. 'Get on' is used for buses and trains, or for relationships, not for clothes.
- **A2-LEX-006**: Your friend says, "Have a nice weekend!" What do you say?
  - Yanıtınız: Me too!
  - Doğru yanıt: You too!
  - 'You too!' wishes the same thing back to the other person. 'Me too!' would mean you also have a nice weekend. 'Yes, please!' answers an offer. 'I'm fine!' answers 'How are you?'.
- **A2-CLZ-002**: I can't hear the music. Can you make it ___, please?
  - Yanıtınız: higher
  - Doğru yanıt: louder
  - We use 'louder' for the volume of a sound. 'Higher' describes the pitch of a note, not its volume. 'Stronger' is used for things like coffee or wind. 'Bigger' describes size, not sound.
- **A2-ERR-002**: Which sentence is correct?
  - Yanıtınız: I am enjoy playing tennis on Sundays.
  - Doğru yanıt: I enjoy playing tennis on Sundays.
  - 'Enjoy' is followed by the -ing form, as in 'enjoy playing'. 'Enjoy to play' wrongly uses 'to' + verb, 'enjoy play' uses the base form, and 'am enjoy' adds 'am' before a present simple verb.
- **A2-CLZ-001**: Yesterday I ___ my keys, so I couldn't open the front door.
  - Yanıtınız: lose
  - Doğru yanıt: lost
  - 'Yesterday' is a finished time, so we use the past simple 'lost'. 'Have lost' is present perfect, which is not used with a finished time. 'Was losing' describes an action in progress, not a single event. 'Lose' is a present form.
- **A1-LEX-002**: Excuse me, can I ___ you a question?
  - Yanıtınız: say
  - Doğru yanıt: ask
  - We 'ask' a question. 'Say' is not followed directly by a person. 'Tell' is used with information or a story, not a question. 'Speak' needs 'to' before a person and is not used with 'a question'.
- **A1-ERR-003**: Look! The children [are] [play] [in] [the] garden.
  - Yanıtınız: the
  - Doğru yanıt: play
  - 'Look!' shows the action is happening now, so we need the present continuous: 'are playing'. 'Are' is correct with 'the children'. 'In' is the right preposition for a garden. 'The' is correct because we know which garden.

### Yanlış yanıtladığınız sorular
_Açıklamalar İngilizcedir._
- **B1-CLZ-007**: She ___ a lot of mistakes in her first English essay.
  - Yanıtınız: had
  - Doğru yanıt: made
  - We 'make' mistakes. 'Did' is common with tasks and homework, but not with 'mistakes'. 'Had' and 'took' do not go with 'mistakes'.
- **B2-ERR-004**: Which sentence is correct?
  - Yanıtınız: You should have tell me you were coming.
  - Doğru yanıt: You should have told me you were coming.
  - Criticism of a past action uses 'should have' + past participle: 'should have told'. 'Should told' leaves out 'have', 'should have tell' uses the base form, and 'should had told' puts a past form after the modal.
- **B2-LEX-004**: You need to ask your manager for a day off next week. Which request is most appropriate?
  - Yanıtınız: Give me Friday off, I really need it.
  - Doğru yanıt: Would it be possible for me to take Friday off?
  - The key politely asks permission with 'Would it be possible...?'. 'I'm taking Friday off' informs instead of asking. 'Give me Friday off' sounds like an order. 'I demand' is aggressive in a request to a manager.
- **B2-CLZ-004**: By the time we got to the cinema, the film ___, so we missed the beginning.
  - Yanıtınız: has started
  - Doğru yanıt: had started
  - The film started before we arrived, so we need the past perfect: 'had started'. 'Would start' refers to a later time. 'Has started' is present perfect, which does not fit a past story. 'Was starting' would mean we arrived just in time.
- **B1-RDG-003** (Library notice): What is the main purpose of the notice?
  - Yanıtınız: To ask people to take part in a survey
  - Doğru yanıt: To tell people about changes at the library
  - The notice describes several changes: Sunday opening, shorter Wednesday hours and a new club. The survey has already happened. The library is not closing; it closes an hour earlier on one day. The reading club is for teenagers, not adults.
- **B1-CLZ-002**: It's raining ___, so take an umbrella and a warm coat.
  - Yanıtınız: strongly
  - Doğru yanıt: heavily
  - 'Rain heavily' is the natural collocation. 'Strongly' is used with wind or opinions, not rain. 'Hardly' means 'almost not', the opposite of the intended meaning. 'Highly' means 'very' before some adjectives, as in 'highly likely'.
- **B1-CLZ-006**: The ___ of the city has grown quickly in the last ten years.
  - Yanıtınız: populate
  - Doğru yanıt: population
  - After 'the' and before 'of' we need a noun: 'population' means the number of people living somewhere. 'Populate' is a verb, 'popular' is an adjective meaning liked by many people, and 'populated' is a participle.
- **A2-CLZ-005**: I'm interested ___ learning Spanish.
  - Yanıtınız: at
  - Doğru yanıt: in
  - 'Interested' is followed by 'in': 'interested in learning Spanish'. 'On', 'at' and 'to' do not follow 'interested'.
- **A2-CLZ-004**: I usually ___ my homework after dinner.
  - Yanıtınız: make
  - Doğru yanıt: do
  - We 'do' homework, just as we do exercises or housework. 'Make' is used when we create something, such as a cake or a plan. 'Take' and 'have' do not go with 'homework' in this sentence.

- **B2-RDG-001** (Gardens between buildings): What is the writer's main point about community gardens?

  - Doğru yanıt: Their social benefits last only if responsibility is shared.
  - The text praises the social benefits, then explains that gardens survive when new members take on responsibility. It says the benefits go 'beyond fresh vegetables', residents felt safer rather than less safe, and depending on a few enthusiastic people is the weak model.
- **B2-LEX-001**: The city council has ___ measures to reduce air pollution near schools.
  - Yanıtınız: done
  - Doğru yanıt: taken
  - We 'take measures' to deal with a problem. 'Made' is common with plans or decisions, but not with 'measures'. 'Done' is a general verb that does not go with 'measures'. 'Put' needs 'in place': 'put measures in place'.
- **B2-CLZ-006**: The new law will ___ effect next month.
  - Yanıtınız: make
  - Doğru yanıt: take

- **C1-CLZ-001**: If I ___ that job offer last year, I would be living in another country now.
  - Yanıtınız: accepted
  - Doğru yanıt: had accepted
  - This mixed conditional joins a past condition ('last year') to a present result ('would be living now'), so the if-clause needs 'had accepted'. 'Accepted' would describe an unreal present. 'Would accept' is not used in the if-clause. 'Have accepted' is not a conditional form.
- **B2-ERR-003**: Everyone in my class [have] [already] [handed in] [their] essay.
  - Yanıtınız: handed in
  - Doğru yanıt: have
  - 'Everyone' takes a singular verb: 'Everyone ... has already handed in'. 'Already' is correctly placed before the past participle. 'Handed in' is the right phrasal verb for giving work to a teacher. 'Their' is the normal pronoun after 'everyone'.
