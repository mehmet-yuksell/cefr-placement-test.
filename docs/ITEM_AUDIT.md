1. **Exactly one defensible key**: no option a careful native speaker could argue for.
2. **A rationale for every distractor**: also enforced by `tools/validate_bank.py`.
3. **Vocabulary at or below the item's level** (English Vocabulary Profile-style judgement), except where the word is the construct.

These checks were made without a corpus or level-list lookup, so the vocabulary judgement in particular is provisional.
